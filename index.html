          Save and put it online
          1. Press Save my changes. Your browser downloads the photos plus one file called content.json.
          2. Put the photos in the images folder and content.json next to index.html.
          3. Upload them to GitHub. The live page picks them up on its own.

          Your changes are remembered on this device even if you close the page, so you can stop halfway and come back.
